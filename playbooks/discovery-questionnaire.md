# Discovery questionnaire

[Playbooks](README.md) · Sample template

## 1. Business outcome

- What problem triggers this initiative, and why now?
- Who owns the outcome and who approves the investment?
- What is the current baseline, how is it measured, and what change would justify adoption?
- What happens if no change is made?

## 2. Users and workflow

- Who performs the work today? Walk through one typical case and one exception.
- Where do delays, manual handoffs and errors occur?
- Which steps require human review or formal approval?
- What must remain in the existing workflow?

## 3. Systems and integration

- Which system owns each required data object?
- What APIs, webhooks, batch interfaces and identity services are available?
- How are authentication, SSO, roles and access provisioning handled?
- What are the expected volumes, latency, retries and reconciliation needs?
- Which dependencies need vendor or security-team approval?

## 4. Data, AI and controls

- Is the required data available, reliable and permitted for the use case?
- What privacy, retention, location and audit requirements apply?
- For AI, what evaluation set and failure examples can the customer provide?
- What actions require human approval, and what is the fallback when the system is uncertain or unavailable?

## 5. Delivery and acceptance

- What is the deadline and what drives it?
- Who supplies data, environments, subject-matter expertise and UAT sign-off?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What criteria separate a successful demo, PoC, pilot and production launch?

## Output to agree

| Field | Capture |
|---|---|
| Problem and baseline | Customer wording, metric, source and measurement owner |
| Prioritized use case | User, trigger, workflow and desired outcome |
| Constraints | Data, security, integration, resources and deadline |
| Open decisions | Decision, owner, due date and delivery impact |
| Next step | Deliverable, acceptance criteria, owner and date |
