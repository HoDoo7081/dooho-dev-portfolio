# Implementation handoff checklist

[Playbooks](README.md) · Sample template

## Customer and scope

- [ ] Customer outcome, baseline and measurement owner captured.
- [ ] Sponsor, business owner, technical owner and delivery lead identified.
- [ ] Included use cases, exclusions, assumptions and commercial commitments reconciled.
- [ ] Demo/PoC limitations and remaining implementation work stated.

## Solution and dependencies

- [ ] Architecture and system-of-record ownership described.
- [ ] API/interface contracts, field mapping and identity/SSO requirements agreed.
- [ ] Roles, access, privacy, retention and audit requirements documented.
- [ ] Volumes, latency, failure handling, idempotency and reconciliation reviewed where relevant.
- [ ] Customer data, environments and third-party approvals have owners and due dates.

## Delivery governance

- [ ] Milestones, staffing assumptions and critical dependencies reviewed.
- [ ] RAID log and open decisions transferred to named owners.
- [ ] Change request, escalation and communication processes agreed.
- [ ] UAT scenarios, test data, defect severity and acceptance authority confirmed.
- [ ] Training, documentation and user enablement responsibilities assigned.

## Launch and operations

- [ ] Launch readiness and go/no-go criteria agreed.
- [ ] Migration/cutover, rollback and customer communication plans assigned where applicable.
- [ ] Monitoring, support hours, escalation paths and incident ownership established.
- [ ] Hypercare period and transition-to-support criteria defined.
- [ ] Adoption/outcome review date and measurement owner agreed.

## Handoff acceptance

| Item | Owner | Evidence / link | Open issue | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requirements | Assign | Approved scope | Record | Agree |
| Technical readiness | Assign | Architecture/interface review | Record | Agree |
| Delivery plan | Assign | Milestones and dependencies | Record | Agree |
| Acceptance and support | Assign | UAT and operational plan | Record | Agree |

The receiving delivery lead confirms what is ready and explicitly accepts or escalates outstanding gaps before kickoff.
