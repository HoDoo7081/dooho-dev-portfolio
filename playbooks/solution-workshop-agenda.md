# 60-minute solution workshop

[Playbooks](README.md) · Sample template

## Purpose and preparation

Agree on one customer problem, assess a solution hypothesis and leave with explicit decisions and owners. Invite a business sponsor, process owner, representative user, technical/integration owner and the delivery lead. Request the current workflow, questions and available baseline data in advance.

| Time | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Confirm outcome, participants and decision authority | Shared objective |
| 5–15 min | Walk through current workflow and exceptions | Current-state problem and constraints |
| 15–25 min | Prioritize one use case and define success | Use case, baseline and proposed measure |
| 25–40 min | Present the solution hypothesis and focused demo | Feedback against the customer's workflow |
| 40–50 min | Review data, integration, permissions and gaps | Feasibility questions and dependencies |
| 50–60 min | Confirm decisions, next steps and owners | Decision log and agreed follow-up |

## Facilitation prompts

- Can the user complete the intended task with this flow?
- Which part is demonstrated, assumed or dependent on further implementation?
- What is a must-have versus a later enhancement?
- What evidence would resolve the remaining uncertainty?

## Follow-up record

| Decision / action | Rationale or evidence | Owner | Due date | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fill after workshop | Link to permitted supporting material | Named owner | Agreed date | Observable completion |

Send the agreed problem, scope, decisions and open questions to participants. Assign unresolved items explicitly and confirm the next delivery or PoC gate.
