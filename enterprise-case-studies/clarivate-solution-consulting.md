# Clarivate — enterprise solution consulting and delivery handoff

[All cases](README.md) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

## Context

As a Technical Solution Consultant, I worked with enterprise customers and internal sales, product, technical and delivery teams to clarify requirements, demonstrate solutions and prepare implementation handoffs.

## My contribution

- Conducted enterprise discovery and translated business needs into solution discussions.
- Prepared and delivered demonstrations and proof-of-concept engagements around customer requirements.
- Clarified data, integration and SSO requirements with customers and relevant technical specialists.
- Supported service scoping and communicated requirements, assumptions and outstanding questions for implementation handoff.
- Explained technical considerations to business audiences and coordinated customer expectations with global teams.
- Delivered several customer AI briefings as interest in AI grew, emphasizing data readiness and customization to the intended use case.

## How I connected discovery to delivery

**Understand the operating problem.** Discovery established what the customer wanted to accomplish, the current workflow and the constraints that would affect a proposed solution. I used this context to focus demonstrations and technical follow-up.

**Make dependencies visible.** Data, integrations and identity requirements could change what was feasible and what implementation would involve. I clarified these with the appropriate specialists and communicated implications to customer and commercial stakeholders.

**Prepare a usable handoff.** Service scoping and implementation handoff connected customer expectations with delivery needs. My contribution was translating and coordinating requirements; I do not claim to have personally built every integration or configured every identity system discussed.

## AI readiness conversations

My message to customers was that successful adoption depends on well-organized data and adaptation to the actual workflow. I explained why a generic AI demonstration alone does not establish readiness for enterprise deployment. These were advisory briefings, not claims of production AI model development.

## Evidence limits

No sales conversion rate, contract value or AI deployment outcome is claimed here. Customer names, PoC datasets and proprietary presentation materials are omitted.
