# Johnson & Johnson — digital transformation and enablement

[All cases](README.md) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

## Context

As a Medical Excellence Specialist, I supported digital transformation and product adoption in Korea while working with regional teams. The work combined AI platform adoption, CRM/ERP implementation activities, stakeholder coordination and regulated medical engagement operations.

## AI platform adoption

- Served as Korea data-workstream lead for an AI-powered customer-engagement platform across eight Asian markets.
- Supported data validation, UAT, launch and diagnostic analysis with regional product and data-science teams.
- Analyzed usage telemetry, gathered user feedback and identified content and CRM workflow issues.
- Coordinated remediation and enablement; AI-suggestion utilization in the Korean Medical Affairs user group rose from approximately 25–30% to over 80%.

The utilization figure describes the relevant Korean user group, not all eight markets. My role focused on data, rollout and adoption rather than training the underlying AI model.

## CRM implementation and workflow alignment

- Supported CRM-related implementation and adoption work by clarifying user workflows, validating data and participating in UAT and issue follow-up.
- Connected local users' needs with regional technical and product stakeholders.
- Used training and feedback to address workflow friction after launch.

## ERP implementation and change management

- Led change management for an SAP ECC-to-Ariba implementation across four Korean affiliates.
- Coordinated workflow requirements, interface and data validation, UAT, defect resolution, training and post-launch adoption.
- Helped connect local operational needs with the implementation team's delivery work.

This describes business-side change management and implementation support; it does not imply ownership of SAP module configuration, ABAP development or the overall regional ERP architecture.

## Workshops and regulated medical engagement

- Planned and operated approximately 30–40 medical events and advisory board meetings annually.
- Coordinated participants, materials, schedules and operational requirements under pharmaceutical-industry constraints.
- Maintained attendance above 95% and reported satisfaction above 95%.

The satisfaction figure is described as satisfaction rather than a standardized Net Promoter Score because the public summary does not establish the survey methodology. Event participation metrics are separate from software-adoption metrics.

## Transferable capabilities

Digital transformation · business-side CRM/ERP implementation · AI adoption · data validation · UAT · change management · training · regulated operations · regional stakeholder alignment.
