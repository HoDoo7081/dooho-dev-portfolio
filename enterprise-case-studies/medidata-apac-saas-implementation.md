# Medidata — APAC enterprise SaaS implementation

[All cases](README.md) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

## Context and scope

I managed enterprise SaaS implementations and customer programs in the regulated life-sciences environment across APAC. Delivery involved customer stakeholders, global delivery and technical specialists, with attention to validation, acceptance and launch readiness.

Across my portfolio, I supported 50+ customer programs and approximately 25–30 new implementations, managing up to 11 projects concurrently. These are cumulative portfolio figures, not the scope of a single engagement.

## My contribution

- Managed scope, schedule, budget, resources, risks and dependencies through implementation and go-live.
- Clarified customer requirements with delivery specialists and translated them into actionable plans and responsibilities.
- Coordinated validation, UAT, issue follow-up and launch readiness with customer and internal teams.
- Escalated unresolved decisions and delivery risks across customer and global stakeholders.
- Managed Professional Services labor forecasting, maintaining approximately 97% monthly forecast accuracy.
- Ran quarterly face-to-face consulting and training sessions for assigned customers, collecting questions beforehand and preparing targeted materials.

## Delivery approach

**Plan around acceptance.** I connected the customer's desired outcome with scope, milestones, responsibilities and the work needed to reach UAT and go-live. Technical questions were worked through with the relevant specialists rather than left as ambiguous delivery commitments.

**Coordinate the portfolio.** With multiple parallel implementations, I balanced resource needs and deadlines, tracked risks and dependencies, and brought decisions to the people who could resolve them. Forecasting made future delivery demand visible alongside current project work.

**Carry implementation into adoption.** Customer sessions began with submitted questions, followed by tailored materials, training and consultation. This kept enablement grounded in customers' actual use and operational needs.

## Results and evidence limits

The portfolio scale and approximately 97% forecast accuracy describe my delivery responsibilities and planning performance. I do not publish customer-level revenue, adoption uplift, defect rates or delivery-time improvements where substantiating data is not available for public use.
