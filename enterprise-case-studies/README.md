## Scope and attribution

- These are retrospective, self-reported summaries of professional experience, not independently audited case studies.
- Portfolio-wide counts describe cumulative work, not one customer or one project. Approximate figures remain labeled as approximate.
- I distinguish coordination, change management and validation from software development and solution configuration. I do not claim sole ownership of team outcomes.
- No customer identities, customer data, screenshots, internal templates, proprietary configurations or employer source code are published.
- The linked playbooks are newly authored examples of my approach. They are not copies of documents used by former employers.
